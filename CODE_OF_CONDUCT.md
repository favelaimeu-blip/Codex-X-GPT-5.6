# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Accept feedback and correct mistakes in good faith.
- Protect private information and never publish another person's credentials, account data, or private logs.
- Respect project scope, maintainer decisions, and the time of other contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized content, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, impersonation, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Serious or repeated incidents can be reported privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially as practical.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces, including public situations where someone is representing the project.
